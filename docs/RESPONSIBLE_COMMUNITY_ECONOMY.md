# Responsible Community Economy

## Intent

UAF should create economic opportunity while behaving as a responsible corporation.

The goal is not extraction. The goal is a fair ecosystem where contributors,
builders, customers, partners, and the company all benefit from trusted,
verifiable, signed artifacts.

```text
community value -> verified artifacts -> fair monetization -> enterprise trust
```

## Corporate responsibility principle

UAF must monetize responsibly.

That means:

- no misleading compliance claims
- no hidden appropriation of community work
- clear artifact ownership
- clear contributor licensing
- transparent commercial boundaries
- fair attribution
- safety and security review before promotion
- responsible revenue sharing where applicable
- enterprise customers receive verified, supportable artifacts
- community members see a path from learning to income

## Economic progression with responsibility

| Stage | Community role | Value created | Responsible company obligation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Learn | Learner | Attention, adoption, future skill | Free access to core learning materials |
| Use | User | Feedback, validation, adoption | Clear docs, no lock-in, honest limits |
| Contribute | Contributor | Docs, schemas, tests, examples | Attribution, transparent license, review process |
| Build | Builder | Policies, reports, dashboards, connectors | Artifact ownership clarity and verification path |
| Verify | Verified Builder | Signed reusable artifacts | Fair review criteria and no arbitrary gatekeeping |
| Monetize | Marketplace Seller | Paid artifacts/services | Revenue terms, payout process, dispute process |
| Partner | Delivery Partner | Customer implementation | Partner standards, support, co-selling rules |
| Enterprise | Enterprise Provider | Production assurance | SLA, security, compliance evidence, accountability |

## Responsible monetization rules

Every monetized artifact must declare:

```text
who owns it
who authored it
who maintains it
what license applies
what support is included
what claims are made
what claims are not made
how it was reviewed
how it can be verified
how revenue is shared if applicable
```

## Artifact fairness rules

UAF should not take a community artifact and sell it without clear rights.

Accepted paths:

1. contributor donates under the project license,
2. contributor publishes a free artifact with attribution,
3. contributor publishes a paid artifact under marketplace terms,
4. contributor contracts with the company for enterprise packaging,
5. contributor joins a partner/customer delivery engagement.

## Compliance responsibility

UAF may say:

```text
SOC 2 aligned
GDPR aligned
control-mapped
evidence-emitting
audit-ready support
```

UAF must not claim:

```text
SOC 2 certified
GDPR guaranteed
compliance automatic
legally sufficient in every deployment
```

unless backed by the proper audit, legal review, or certification process.

## Responsible enterprise boundary

Enterprise customers pay for:

- verified artifacts
- supported builds
- signed bundles
- control mappings
- deployment assurance
- evidence workflows
- security response
- support obligations
- operational maturity

They should not pay merely to access the basic truth layer of the protocol.

## Community protection

The community should always have access to:

```text
protocol
basic runtime
basic replay
basic evidence model
basic policy examples
basic reports
basic Kubernetes run path
```

This preserves trust and makes the ecosystem credible.

## Company protection

The company may protect:

```text
brand
trademarks
certification marks
managed cloud
enterprise UX
premium policy packs
premium report packs
private connectors
customer deployment methods
commercial support playbooks
```

## Responsible corporation commitments

UAF as a company should commit to:

1. open core remains usable,
2. contributors are credited,
3. commercial terms are clear,
4. security issues are handled responsibly,
5. compliance claims are precise,
6. customer data is protected,
7. community artifacts are not misrepresented,
8. verified artifacts are reviewed fairly,
9. monetization supports maintainers and builders,
10. enterprise trust is earned through evidence, not marketing.

## Economic flywheel

```text
open core builds trust
trust grows community
community creates artifacts
artifacts become verified
verified artifacts support enterprise adoption
enterprise revenue funds core, maintainers, and partners
stronger core grows more community
```

## Final rule

Monetize everything responsibly.

That means every artifact has a value path, but not every artifact must be closed.

The strongest company is the one that makes the ecosystem richer, safer, and more trustworthy while still capturing enough value to sustain the mission.
